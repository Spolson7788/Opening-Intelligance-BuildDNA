per-call ceiling rather than relying on an expected price. Each completed call
settles its reservation before the next paid call. Lost responses retain their
ceiling and pause the trial. Complete account-limit rejections stop later sends
and become `rejected_uncharged_pending_review`; a staging admin can reconcile
only an eligible rejection with recorded evidence. Review does not reopen a trial.
